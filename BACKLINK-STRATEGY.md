# IntellDev backlink strategy

## Goal
Build relevant, trustworthy links to `https://intelldev.tn/` that support local discovery in Tunis and wider Tunisia without buying links or using automated link networks.

## Priority opportunities

1. Create or complete profiles on Google Business Profile, LinkedIn Company Page, Behance, Dribbble, Clutch, Sortlist, and relevant Tunisian business directories. Keep the name, URL, email, and service descriptions consistent.
2. Publish two useful case studies per month: the problem, decisions, deliverables, and measurable outcome. Link each case study back to the main site and share it with the client for a natural portfolio or partner mention.
3. Build partnerships with Tunisian founders, photographers, copywriters, developers, coworking spaces, and startup communities. Offer a useful workshop or co-created resource, then request an editorial mention where relevant.
4. Pitch practical French-language contributions to Tunisian business, entrepreneurship, design, and marketing publications. Prioritize articles that answer a real audience question and earn a contextual link.
5. Submit the site to relevant local events, incubators, chambers, and professional networks only when IntellDev genuinely participates.

## Quality rules

- Prefer a small number of relevant editorial links over large directory volumes.
- Never buy links, exchange links at scale, use private blog networks, or publish spun content.
- Use descriptive, natural anchor text such as `studio digital à Tunis` or `IntellDev`, not repeated exact-match keywords.
- Track referring domain, URL, date, target page, anchor text, and whether the link is still live each month.

## Measurement

Track branded searches, referring domains, qualified referral visits, contact-form submissions, and visibility for `studio digital Tunis`, `création site web Tunis`, and related service terms in Search Console and analytics. Review the plan quarterly and keep only channels that produce relevant visits or credible relationships.
